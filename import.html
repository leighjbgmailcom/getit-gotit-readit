Import Your Library

Already keep a spreadsheet of your books? Import it here instead of adding titles one at a time.

CSV format

Your file needs a header row with at least these columns (extra columns are fine and ignored):

Author, Book Title, Status, Year

Status should be one of: Get It, Got It (or Got It - Paper / Got It - Kindle / etc.), or Read It. Rows missing a title or a recognizable status are skipped automatically.

Note: imported books are cached directly from your file (title, author, year) rather than matched against Open Library, so covers show as placeholders for now. Re-adding a favorite later via Search will pick up its real cover.
