Jordan Oldtimers Hockey League Constitution

The official rules and governance of the JOHL — membership, the League Executive, playing rules, discipline, standings, playoffs and the draft.

Purpose of Constitution

The constitution depicts the rules by which the Jordan Oldtimers Hockey League (JOHL) will operate and the restrictions, powers, and duties of its Executive. The constitution will be used to help promote, supervise, conduct, and control the operation of the league.

The Jordan Oldtimers Hockey League

The JOHL was founded for a safe place for hockey players to enjoy the game they love. The League is run by volunteer members. The league currently operates with 6 teams of players 35+ where teams are determined by a draft prior to each new season.

Membership

Any person who has paid the required annual fee for that year will be deemed a member for that year. Failure to make payments at the prescribed times will result in a forfeiture of membership.

The League Executive reserves the right to expel any member whose conduct on or off the ice is deemed detrimental to the league.

Any player, who is absent for an extended period of time due to an injury, will be given the first opportunity to re-enter the league. Refunds based on the number of games played may be given due to long-term injury or other extenuating circumstances. (this is discussed below in further detail)

The captains from each team each year will be designated as part of the “League Committee”. This committee will have a role in aiding the executive committee with the yearly operations of the league and will have voting privileges on certain items.

League Executive (Members, Duties, Responsibilities)

Executive Members

The executive may consist of 3 full-time positions. President, Vice-President, and Treasurer.

Executive Duties

  • To manage the operation of the league as laid out in the league’s constitution
  • To set and administer league policies
  • To rule on matters that arise not covered in the constitution

Terms of Office

  • The League Executive will consist of volunteers from the current league membership and approval of the current Executive
  • If an Executive position becomes vacant and more than one person has volunteered for the position, the chosen candidate will be based on a vote with current Executive and team reps (each team can have 3 reps with 1 vote per person and if said person is also a full-time executive member, only 1 vote shall be submitted.

Voting Procedures

  • A quorum of 4 of the 6 teams + the 1 member of the League Executive is required to conduct voting procedures
  • At least 1 member from each team must be present to vote (video/phone call in during voting is accepted)
  • A simple majority is necessary for passage of any league matters

Responsibilities

The President

  • Preside at all meetings of The Executive
  • Call and arrange meetings of The Executive
  • Act as a spokesperson for the league
  • Oversee the operation of the league
  • Act as a second signing authority on all league cheques
  • Assign duties to team reps
  • Arrange and Manage Player Draft, spare list
  • Arrange League Ice time slots
  • Final Arbiter of discipline (unless involved or team was involved)

The Vice-President

  • Aid The President in league oversight and week to week operations
  • Perform the duties of The President in their absence
  • Assign responsibilities for league stats, website and timekeepers
  • Arrange for and co-ordinate league referees
  • Arrange uniforms for teams
  • Arbiter of Discipline when the President is unable

The Treasurer

  • General Financial Oversight
  • Deposit all league money received into the league bank account monitored by the League Executive
  • Authorize all league payments and provide first signature on all league cheques
  • Prepare annual budget
  • Prepare Financial statement at year-end and in the interim as requested by the Executive
  • Provide financials at the beginning of draft night
  • Arrange for liability insurance for the league

League Committee

  • Aid the Executive in operating the league
  • Ensure their individual teams follow the code of conduct
  • Vote on constitutional changes and league happenings that do not go straight to the Executive
  • Vote on yearly league awards

PLAYING RULES

The Jordan Oldtimers Hockey League (JOHL) is owned and operated by its league members and it’s executive. The league plays ice hockey under the rules of the Canadian Hockey Association (CHA) official rule book with these additional rules included:

CODE OF CONDUCT (on and off the ice): All teams and all players agree to do everything in their power to play hockey in a sportsmanlike manner and display good will towards their opponent and all fellow league officials. The league is open to players looking for a fun, competitive and safe environment to play a recreational brand of organized hockey. The on-ice officials are to eject all players who do not uphold the league code of conduct and report such violation to the League President.

REFUND POLICY: The League rents its ice from the Town of Lincoln and commits to building its league schedule-based player commitments and their subsequent payments. All players participating take on the risk of injury and as such there will be no refund after the first 5 weeks of the season. For any refund request prior to the 5-week mark, a special request may be submitted and reviewed by the League Executive on a case-by-case basis.

Any delay or ban of playing games over any portion of a season by any sanctioned body, including health authorities, government or by an act of god, shall under all circumstances delay the scheduled games and or season until such delay or ban has been lifted. The JOHL shall credit players for any lost game or games that can not be played due to unavailable ice and or the fact that the season can not be extended. Such credit can be paid out to the player(s) at the end of the season or can remain as a credit towards the following season.

UNIFORM POLICY: All players must wear their team’s full uniform of jersey and socks, or their team may be penalized. Goalies may be given an exemption pending approval from the League Executive.

  • 1st offence is a warning
  • 2nd and subsequent offences will be a minor penalty each game for their respective team

PENALTY INFRACTIONS: Players who participate in the JOHL shall only be permitted 3 minor penalties per game before they will receive an automatic game ejection. A 7- minute major (run time) will be an automatic game ejection as per the CHA rules of hockey and may face supplementary discipline as decided by the league executive.

7-minute Major – will be reviewed by the League Executive to determine if further disciplinary action is required. (fighting addressed below)

7-Minute Match – automatic 5 game suspension and potentially more pending a review by League Executive. If it is the 2nd offence for a match penalty, they will be expelled from the league. A return the following year will have to be reviewed by the League Executive and the League Committee.

Fighting – automatic 3 games suspension. The 2nd offence for this, the player will be expelled from the league for the remainder of the year pending a review by League Executive. A return the following year will have to be reviewed by the League Executive and the League Committee.

The league will not permit players taking multiple ROUGHING, SLASHING, HI-STICKING, ELBOWING, CROSS CHECKING or other aggressive type of penalties. These penalties are considered to be against our CODE OF CONDUCT and players who continually take such infractions will be called in under review by the Executive who have the right to suspend those players playing privileges without refund. Players agree to uphold the leagues code of conduct and have no recourse should they violate it.

*Slapshots will NOT be permitted *

Knocking down the puck with a high stick will not be a penalty unless it was deemed to also be above the shoulders.

Minor Penalty – 3 mins run time

Major Penalty – 7 mins run time

BODY CONTACT- No Body Checking is permitted. A minimum of a minor penalty will be assessed for a Body Contact penalty and potentially more.

GAME FORMAT: All games shall consist of (3) 20-minute periods of running time. The last 2 minutes of the 3rd period will be stop time when the game is within 2 goals. During the regular season only, there will be no ties. If a game is tied, 1 player from each team will be picked to go in a sudden death shootout. Process repeats until a winner is determined. Same player cannot shoot twice unless all other players have gone.

ICING – The defending zone blue line will be used as the icing line.

Power Play Face Offs – when a new penalty is issued, the immediate face off that follows will be in the penalized teams end zone.

Spare Players – A team may ask another player from another team in the JOHL to spare for them if required to make 7 skaters or less. A spare cannot be used to make 8 or more skaters.

TEAM CAPTAIN: Each team will have 3 Captains and potentially 1 playing sponsor that are protected and slotted into the draft. These team ‘captains’ will take care of all week to week handling of their team and will ensure their teams behavior and sportsmanship follow the leagues guidelines. Team Captains will contact their team members shortly after the draft to inform them of the team that has selected them and to provide each member of their team the contact information for their captains.

Pre-printed game sheets will be printed each week, but it is recommended for team captains to scratch out any players off the list that they know are not coming for that game before it gets to the timekeeper.

If your team has 1 of your goalies that can also play out, you may dress them as a player only if your team has 9 skaters or less. Then they can play out to give your team 10 full skaters.

Team Captains will not allow any non-member of the JOHL to play a game unless approved by the league President. This is very rare and would generally be a goalie. All goalies from the league must be asked to play first before going outside the league for a goalie.

The 3 captains for each team are also a part of the League Committee each season.

ARENA POLICY

All teams are to adhere to the Arena Policies of the facility in which they are playing in including all Provincial Health Orders and Federal Regulated laws. Players are to conduct themselves in a Sportsmanship manner, be courteous to all members of the JOHL including on and off ice officials, League Executive, other arena users including the public and all arena staff. Any member contravening this may be removed from the league or face suspension by the league executive. Anyone violating Arena Policy including racist remarks will be reported to the League President and shall face expulsion immediately.

SUSPENSIONS

Players under suspension may watch their team play, help coach or just be around the team provided they follow the league code of conduct. Any player currently under suspension getting involved in an altercation on or off the ice may be removed from the league.

The League Executive will determine the length of suspensions and have the authorization to remove a player from the league permanently if it is deemed necessary.

RISK OF INJURY

All players understand that playing the game of ice hockey comes with a high risk of injury and even death. All players play at their own risk by taking part in sanctioned games in the JOHL.

STANDINGS

During the regular season each team will be awarded the following:

Win – 2 points

Tie – (no ties for regular season)

Shootout Loss – 1 point

These points will be used to determine the playoff point system (discussed below) going into the playoffs.

In the event of a tie in the regular season standings, the following will be used to determine seeding in order;

  • Head-to-Head
  • Wins
  • Goals Against
  • Goals For

PLAYOFF POINT SYSTEM AND BREAK DOWN

The Playoffs will start with a 5-game round robin against each team. There are no shootouts in the playoffs so teams will be awarded 2 points for a win and 1 for a tie. All teams make the playoffs.

The Regular season standings will decide the following breakdown of points that teams will start the playoffs with;

1st place – 1.5 pts

2nd place – 1 pt

3rd place – 0.75 pts

4th place – 0.5 pts

5th place – 0 pts

6th place – 0 pts

After the round robin is complete, the top 4 teams in the playoff standings will move on to play on the final championship night as follows;

3rd vs 4th – Consolation Final

**If tied at the end of regulation, a 10-minute sudden death 5 on 5 overtime will be played. If still tied after that, a shoot-out will take place with the same rules as the regular season

1st vs 2nd – Championship Game

** If tied at the end of regulation, a sudden death 5 on 5 overtime will be played until a champion is determined. Period(s) will be 20 minutes.

Tie Breaker in the playoffs used to determine the final night teams will be;

  • Head-to-Head
  • Wins
  • Goals Against
  • Goals For

LEAGUE AWARDS

Each year the league will recognize players on the following individual awards;

  • League MVP
  • Top Defenceman
  • Top Goalie
  • Rookie of the Year
  • Gentleman of the Year

All league awards will be voted on by 3 team captains (1 vote each) from each team near the end of the regular season to determine the winners.

DRAFT NIGHT

Each Team will consist of 2 goalies and 15 skaters

To begin each season a draft will be conducted to determine the teams. Teams will start with 3 Captains and a Sponsor and then choose the rest on draft night. Before the draft begins, the captains will all come to a consensus as to what round each captain and sponsor will be slotted in. This will generally be done in August at a captains meeting held before the draft.

On the night of the draft, any new players to the league will be discussed and information passed along fairly as to their playing ability if known.

To begin the draft a deck of cards will be used to determine the draft. Highest Cards (Ace High) will determine the draft order from 1-6. The draft is a snake drafting system until each section is complete (goalies then players). This will be done for goalies first and then new cards will be drawn to determine the order for players selection.

At the end of the draft, as soon as the last pick is made, a 0-minute timer will start, and teams will be allowed to make trades. Once that time elapses, there will be no more trades throughout the rest of the season.

If the league did not fill all player spots, the draft will continue until all registered players are drafted. As the next player(s) join the league, the remaining teams will be put into a hat and a random draw will take place to determine what team(s) the new player(s) will be placed on.

NOTE: At least 2 of the League Executive must be present when the draw is conducted.

There will be a deadline issued yearly in a league email to allow all returning players the first chance to return. After that date passes, the league will open up to new players.

NOTE: players that have not paid the minimum pre draft deposit of $200 before draft night will not be drafted and risk losing their spot in the league.
